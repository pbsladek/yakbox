# Reference voice provenance

These twenty-five prompt clips are derived from LibriVox recordings and are bundled
for local Chatterbox testing. LibriVox states that all of its recordings are
public domain in the United States and may be modified or used commercially.
Copyright status can differ outside the United States.

The clips do not imply that any recorded reader, LibriVox, or the authors of
the source works endorse Yakbox or speech generated with Yakbox. The complete
reader list is recorded in the adjacent registry.

[`voices.toml`](voices.toml) is the machine-readable source of truth. It records
the original recording URL and checksum, the extracted interval, audio format,
bundled-file checksum, reader page, catalog page, and public-domain policy URL.

The approved media policy for this example accepts public-domain material,
CC0, and CC BY 4.0. It rejects unknown rights, noncommercial restrictions, and
no-derivatives restrictions. Software in Yakbox follows the repository's
permissive software-license policy separately.
